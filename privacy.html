InVEST Privacy Policy

Effective December 1, 2026 · InVEST version 1.0

InVEST is the companion app to How to Set Goals and Conquer Them by Louis Apalucci. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your goals stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type into InVEST stays on your own device and is never sent to us. That includes your name, age, goals, Vision, WHY, Plan, daily progress, notes, and reflections. There are no accounts, and we cannot see, read, or recover your goals.

What we count

We may count anonymous events, such as how many people opened the app, started a goal, finished a Goal Sheet, or tapped a link to the book, and where they found the app (for example, "from the book" or "from a shared card"). These counts never include your name, your goal, anything you typed, your contacts, or your location, and they can't be used to identify you. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Sharing

When you tap Share, InVEST hands a progress card or message to your phone's own share menu. You choose where it goes. Nothing is shared unless you share it.

Links to other sites

The "Get the book" links open Amazon or our Goals website. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Amazon may record that you arrived from InVEST so the author can see which links lead to book sales.

Children

InVEST is made for goal setters of all ages, including students. Because the app collects no personal information and has no accounts, children can use it without sharing personal data with us. If we add accounts in the future, we will update this policy and follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) before collecting anything from children under 13.

Backups and deleting your data

You can save a backup file from Settings. You control that file. To delete everything, delete a goal from its sheet, or uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions? Contact Louis Apalucci through louisapalucci.weebly.com/goals.
